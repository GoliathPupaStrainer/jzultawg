## About the product

**Guitar Pro Full** is a tablature editor with playback for guitar and bass. On Windows 10 and 11 people reach for it for writing and learning songs — when you want one stable app on the desktop instead of juggling trial pop-ups.

This page is a short orientation: what the tool does, what is already unlocked in this build, and what to check first if the app does not open as expected.

**What is open in this build**

Full playback, export, and score tools from the retail app are enabled. The same modules and menus people pay for in the store build are available here; you do not need to unlock extras inside the app.
